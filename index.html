Daylist

A quiet, ambient daily planner. Write your day the way you’d say it out loud, Daylist works out the time and the repeat.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Plan in plain words “Gym at 7am every day”, “call mum friday”. Daylist reads it the way you’d say it.
  • Reminders that mean it A quiet nudge, or a real alarm, your choice, task by task.
  • Everything stays on your device No account, no server, no analytics. Your plan is yours.
